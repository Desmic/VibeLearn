# First composition checkpoint

The arrival view is framed by a deep civic arcade on the left and water on the right. A continuous warm stone promenade bends past a garden lightwell to a reachable raised overlook. Thick piers, arched openings, recessed doors, a curved roof and planted balconies give the close building volume. A branched pink canopy and a small roofed pavilion hold the overlook. Layered civic structures beyond it read as a wider city, not another playable destination.

The player directly walks a small robot through this place. Mouse or touch orbit changes the view; visible direction controls and a short route button support navigation. This checkpoint ends at the overlook with no story interaction or learning system. Root reviews the actual form in GUI before the interaction loop expands.
