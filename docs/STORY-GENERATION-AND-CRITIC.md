# Story design and rendered-story review

**15 September 2026.** [CRITIC-POLICY.md](CRITIC-POLICY.md) is authoritative. Written treatment reviews identify promise, contradictions, prerequisites and staging risks; they do not pass rendered story quality. Keep StoryWorldSpec renderer-neutral and separate from canonical learning/evidence identity.

Freeze audience assumptions, premise, player role, character desire, important objects, inciting event, causal chain, stakes, meaningful actions, progression, payoff and the concept mapping before realization. The user wants an ongoing series about how LLMs work; use [NEXT-TEACHING-DESIGN.md](NEXT-TEACHING-DESIGN.md).

Review the actual opening for world/role/stakes, visible causality and attachment/forward pull. Use whole-number anchored ratings, no weighted average, and evidence for each criterion. Unobserved means unassessed. Story told in captions over scenery cannot pass because its script sounds compelling. Review the whole playable chapter even if first touch fails.

Preserve user-paced navigation, Back/Skip/Replay, pause while motion runs, reduced-motion meaning and semantic accessibility. Preferences must be explicit, not inferred. The current user is the sole human product critic and final authority.
