## Problem and intended result

The cold reviewer understood a signal and greeting, but did not discover a
distinct destination or understand the partners' contributions. The first action
felt like starting a story sequence. Separately, moving the camera could remove
the controls needed to make that action. These are different failures: attention
and meaning need authored staging; access needs a reusable system guarantee.

The v8 design proposes one receiver inspection after the committed route, not a
second route confirmation. Until inspection the reciprocal result stays put.
Inspection frames the actual destination and a brief personal invitation before
the existing Warden sequence continues. The lower canopy terrace and elevated
transit balcony have different functional silhouettes within the same existing
receiver island. Neither becomes walkable in this slice.

Success requires a player to identify Zip, name a partner's contribution, point
out the place that answered, and explain why they might want to meet there. A
glowing object, extra sentence, concept image or passed schema does not prove it.
The canonical design must pass its exact independent prototype review before
this new interaction is implemented. Existing critic scores do not transfer.
