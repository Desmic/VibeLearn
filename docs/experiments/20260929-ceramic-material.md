## Hypothesis and retained direction

The previous arrival is dominated by dark glass and nearly black bands. The
accepted graphic-utopia reference uses luminous mineral masses, selectively deep
indigo shadows, quieter surfaces and legible layered space. Test this relationship
on actual geometry rather than applying a whole-image filter or adding props.
The first material-only pass was too subtle; the repair changes the existing sun
direction and fill alongside the material balance. It is therefore not a causal
test of palette alone.

User feedback UF-07/08/11/14 keeps original far-future fantasy, freedom, graphic
design with credible lighting and the art-first priority. V3's reuse-first and
conditional-Blender principles mean this pass reuses the exact existing assets;
no additional conditioning was needed. Sky Reach's lesson remains spatial desire
and meaningful exploration, not copying its scale. This finish study cannot prove
that lesson, an inhabited world, story attachment or learning quality. The named
game studies and learning research remain requirements for subsequent experience
work, not claims satisfied by brighter architecture.

## Reusable lesson and next constraint

Judge an asset's material roles under the intended light rig before replacing its
geometry or generating another asset. Inspect at least arrival, inside and reverse
views; preserve a switchable control. Brighter values can reveal existing form but
cannot replace distinctive silhouettes or purposeful world activity. Keep the
profile separate from learning semantics; this experiment does not validate a
universal style-transfer pipeline.

Next visual work should address a specific form/composition deficiency, using
the preserved portal concept and asset-method comparison when resources become
available. Do not grow the map or restart generic detail accumulation.
