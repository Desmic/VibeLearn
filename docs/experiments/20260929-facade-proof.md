# Small architectural proof — 29 September 2026

User authorizes a small commitment after the accumulated visual failures: prove
graphics/art style and credible physical grounding before expanding map size.
Root directly implements one conservatory entrance in the existing isolated
Three.js experiment, selected with `?study=facade`. The previous arcade remains
available unchanged at the default URL and in its frozen source archive.

This is one bounded form-production test, not another wider world or a reset of
the failed arcade's verdict. No learning/story expansion or production changes.
One initial result and at most one focused repair before recording its outcome.

Visible targets before implementation:
- Recognizable contemporary/future civic construction, recessed entrance and
  joined building masses; avoid isolated discs, repeated blades or toy blocks.
- Graphic silhouettes, cream/coral accents and deep blue/teal shadow families,
  with credible directional light, material distinctions and contact shadows.
- One planted foreground, one framed route and an open view beyond. The same
  place must make sense on arrival, in the doorway and looking back.
- Original protagonist remains at human scale. Walking and camera collision
  follow visible structures, with no floating feet or passing through planters.
  This establishes bounded kinematic physicality, not rigid-body simulation.

Reuse existing character, painted foliage, engine geometry facilities, licensed
surface resources and controls. New work is the architectural form/composition.
Sol only audits and verifies engineering; root owns all art implementation/play.
Compare actual views to the accepted reference, preserve failures and measurements.
Do not certify motion/audio quality or a general world generator from this proof.

## Built and observed

Root implemented `facade.ts`: an 18x22 metre finite court, one asymmetric civic
building, deep rounded portal, recessed glazed bays, curved entrance canopy,
upper gallery, sun-screen and three rooted planting beds. This reduces the
scope of the previous arcade instead of adding map area. Existing Zip, painted
foliage, material images and controls are reused. Original scene still selects
at `/`; this study selects at `/?study=facade`.

Initial build `index-BNeVnbtp.js` was observed and saved as
`artifacts/bellweather-arcade/facade-initial-native.png`. Root judged the building
more connected/recognizable, but too smooth and generic. The one scoped repair
adds `graphic-material.ts`: world-attached mineral pigment and cool shade/warm
light colour control on selected physically lit surfaces. It does not process
the whole screen or change vegetation/glass/character shading indiscriminately.
A soft contact patch replaces the visibly circular contact disc in this study.

The character standing height now derives from actual model bounds and the
0.130m tile surface. Vertical bob is disabled here so it does not lift planted
feet artificially. Visible low boxes, wall masses, portal jambs, planter radii
and court bounds supply the horizontal movement limits. A post-smoothing camera
ray from the character pivot checks for occlusion by solid architecture.
These are scoped kinematic checks; there is no rigid-body simulation, terrain
ground probe, foot planting/IK or newly implemented walking animation.

Final-build native evidence under `artifacts/bellweather-arcade/`:
`facade-arrival-native.png`, `facade-reverse-native.png`, `facade-phone-native.png`.
Root used guided traversal plus actual camera input, not a complete manual
collision sweep. Sol's focused held-key checks complement this observation.

Worker art judgment: construction and near-surface depth are more legible than
the prior isolated discs/blades. The footprint is intentionally compact. This
still does not meet the full graphic-utopian reference: the protagonist is
rudimentary, the visual language remains restrained, and the open backdrop is
only a colour field. No claim of a living complete world or user acceptance.
Stop at this small proof rather than decorating it with map/story expansion.

Reusable finding: a visual kit must pair geometry and material response with
visible collision/scale. The mineral shading is an opt-in module; this proof
does not authorize making Bellweather's palette, topology or controller the
general platform. Existing production/learning/save boundaries are untouched.

the view close to the character filled the frame with the head. Sol repaired
only the facade camera/control branch, searching nearby clear orbit angles and
including character-width clearance on alternatives. Input follows the actual
camera bearing after a correction. Root code review caught an in-place vector
normalization error during repair; it was corrected before final verification.
