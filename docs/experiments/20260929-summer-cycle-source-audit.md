## What makes the scene work

| [`src/world/chunks.ts` authored village lots](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/world/chunks.ts#L63-L77), [seeded prototypes](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/world/chunks.ts#L152-L185), [recycled/culled chunks](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/world/chunks.ts#L1000-L1017) | An authored road, village, sightlines, and silhouettes constrain deterministic variation. This is procedural realization under composition rules, not arbitrary generation or an on-demand world model. For VibeLearn, constrain any layout variation by readable relationships, routes, and learning objects before varying dressing. |
| [`src/render/materials.ts` brush and cel light](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/materials.ts#L116-L214), [per-surface branches and specialization](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/materials.ts#L350-L353), [`specializeUber`](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/materials.ts#L703-L762) | Surface-specific palettes, world-anchored brush/weathering, three light bands, shadow tint, and rim are designed together. A geometry scan produces a compile-time mask of only its material branches. The visual language begins in geometry/material/light, rather than a blanket image filter. Specialization also avoids making every leaf pixel pay for unrelated skin/roof branches. |
| [`src/render/leafAtlas.ts`](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/leafAtlas.ts#L5-L8), [instanced vegetation and near/far trees](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/world/chunks.ts#L934-L977), [merged structures](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/world/chunks.ts#L934-L941) | A painted procedural leaf atlas, instancing, merged static meshes and foliage LOD support a dense but coherent scene. | [`src/render/post.ts` multi-target paint/ink](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/post.ts#L13-L21), [Kuwahara and edge masks](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/post.ts#L79-L148) | The post pass uses color, normals/group/line weights, and depth: painted smoothing backs off around near/detail areas, while ink follows depth, normal, and semantic object boundaries. | [`src/main.ts` staged building and warm-up](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/main.ts#L62-L95), [`precompile.ts`](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/precompile.ts#L4-L23), [adaptive MSAA](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/main.ts#L118-L145) | Asset generation and shader/first-draw uploads are staged behind a loader; anti-aliasing is adjusted from measured pacing rather than one low FPS sample. These choices trade startup time and complexity for smooth first play. VibeLearn should measure the actual learner device/path before borrowing the policy. |

The [README reports 61→118 fps](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/README.md#L236-L256) at 1920×1080/4× MSAA on an RTX 4060, with approximately 540 main-scene draws and 2.3 million triangles. This is the author's benchmark, not a measurement from this audit. [`profiler.ts`](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/src/render/profiler.ts#L4-L43) supports GPU timer queries, which is stronger than FPS alone. The author [explicitly says there is no mobile/low-quality mode and the primitive rider falls short up close](https://github.com/StarKnightt/summer-cycle/blob/8b977baad061e797c2f6c19cfcf07c1e79b23a67/README.md#L258-L267). Those limits matter for VibeLearn's phone and character requirements.

**Practical comparison.** VibeLearn's existing [`web/playcanvas-backend.js`](../../web/playcanvas-backend.js) already has material, light/shadow, static-batching, and demand-render boundaries. The fastest relevant transfer is an **authored visual kit and scene-composition benchmark**: stronger silhouettes and spatial contact, a coherent palette/lighting profile, an approved foliage/asset family, and measured draw/texture/frame cost on desktop *and* phone. Reuse PlayCanvas facilities and pinned licensed assets; consider custom paint/outline shaders only after real renderer images show a specific unmet need. Summer Cycle's custom shader stack and bespoke road/chunk logic are evidence of concentrated art direction, not evidence that an engine switch or universal procedural generator would improve this product.
