# Existing-asset daylight comparison: engineering verification

The final comparison used the same optional atelier study at `http://127.0.0.1:8062/?study=facade&portal=crafted&architecture=atelier`, with `&finish=daylight` for the candidate. It remains an experimental visual variant; the root reviewer owns the native art/play judgment.

The served final bundle is `index-Dow4yO3h.js`, SHA-256 `CCA80D3552EB8333BA8A5FE65588F09D016015984C63FAE0C440FA2894D5A5A6`. The conditioned v4 GLB is SHA-256 `0827034DF3A667F8D1F9555E291AA6EEB7B07609FEFC878881FC17EDBF9E80C1` (2,046,188 bytes). The exact source/asset packet, including the Blender conditioner, editable `.blend`, verifier scripts and provenance, is `artifacts/bellweather-arcade/existing-asset-lighting-source.zip`, SHA-256 `9F382B469377EB92613B4D5793EDE6D2FCF71D095043CD4050F4FB3F17B36EC2` (57 files; excludes `node_modules`, `dist`, `.git` and Blender backups).

The initial v3 conditioning was inspected in the native browser, then rejected by geometry verification before acceptance and the final engineering browser comparison: it had 240 fewer triangles, 169 missing unique positions, changed exact normals and an open/nonmanifold edge under the welded-shell check. Its findings remain in `relighting-v3-geometry-comparison.json`. The final v4 uses a welded bake proxy only and transfers UVs to the untouched original. On the **exported** v4 GLB, `verify-atelier-mesh.mjs` found 22,968 valid triangles, zero classified degenerates and 89 closed outward shells. `compare-atelier-geometry.mjs` found exact equality of unique positions, normals, position-normal pairs, position-defined triangle sets, vertex-record count (51,647) and all ten material-role triangle counts against the original GLB. Both geometry records are in `artifacts/bellweather-arcade/relighting-v4-{mesh-qa,geometry-comparison}.json`. The v4 GLB contains one embedded PNG and nine materials reference it as occlusion texture; `src/atelier.ts` selects v4 for `finish=daylight` and sets its AO intensity to 0.7.

`verify-relighting-ab.mjs` visited baseline then candidate in one owned headless Chrome process at 1102×828, DPR 1, ANGLE Intel UHD Graphics / D3D11, and closed the browser. Matched manual movement, orbit, crafted-jamb contact, aperture passage/return, near-wall camera distance (~5.9566 m), pause/restart and 390×844 control bounds passed. Arrival, approach and passage positions differed by 0, 0.017 and 0.044 m. The candidate v4 GLB, camera proxy and both embedded-image blob requests returned HTTP 200; no page, shader, console or resource errors were recorded. Full snapshots and screenshots: `artifacts/bellweather-arcade/relighting-ab-engineering.json` and `relighting-ab-{baseline,candidate}-*.png`.
