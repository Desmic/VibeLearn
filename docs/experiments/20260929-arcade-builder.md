# Bellweather arcade builder — first composition checkpoint

29 September 2026. Isolated source: `experiments/bellweather-arcade`. This is an initial navigable art composition for root GUI review, not an opening candidate or accepted world.

The scene places the player in a sheltered garden arcade with four deep arched bays, recessed doors, planted balconies and a curved constructed roof. A continuous curved stone promenade passes three water basins and branched vegetation to a reachable belvedere with piers, roof, rail, seating and a framed city view. The distant district and sky establish scale without implying another local destination. A small robot is directly controllable with WASD/arrows, visible directional buttons, camera drag and wheel zoom; the route button follows the same walkable path in either direction. Pause stops scene updates; blur clears movement; hidden-tab handling is intended to stop updates but has only a simulated visibility event check. Reduced motion removes the robot walk bob.

The source uses Three.js and the pinned MIT Summer Cycle RNG utility; license and provenance are in the experiment. No source, geometry or architectural vocabulary was taken from the frozen `bellweather-world` trial. The accepted reference image guided form and composition but is not used as a backdrop.

## Functional repair after root's first native play

Root walked/orbited the initial scene and reached the pavilion. The sky was black because its radius was beyond the camera's far plane. The sphere now sits within the frustum, with a blue scene background as fallback. Three.js warnings from an undefined `map` material parameter and removed `PCFSoftShadowMap` were removed. Guided travel previously recalculated its destination from the current route position every frame, making it reverse near the overlook; the destination is now fixed when the route button is activated. Pause, blur, hidden state, manual movement and restart clear that destination. The pause menu makes the underlying walking controls inert, and paused or hidden resize does not start scene rendering.

## First substantial visual repair — ready for root play

One repair reshaped the huge green slab into a finite constructed garden terrace with visible retaining faces and a lower valley. Layered ridge/cloud bands, suspended civic terraces and more detailed stepped towers now sit beyond the reachable pavilion. The core route stays in place; a paved, walkable arrival apron now extends behind the original start. Existing tree branches now support asymmetrical crowns made from folded, individually colored instanced leaves with darker interior and lighter outer growth. Stone and paving have surface-specific grain. The arcade and pavilion gained structural soffit/bracket detail and the pavilion's arrival rail has a central gap. Lighting exposure/ambient was reduced to make cast and contact shadows more legible. Camera occlusion now checks 134 relevant solid architectural meshes instead of scanning every leaf and distant object each frame. Static repeated trim, branch and small plant meshes are batched by material while keeping shadow casting.
