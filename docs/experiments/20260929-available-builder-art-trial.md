The composition should make tangible use of:

- A close civic garden arcade: thick structural piers, curved roof/balcony,
  shaded recesses, believable doors and occupied surfaces. A built place with
  depth, not isolated decorative blades.
- A continuous promenade whose curve reveals the next destination. Water and
  planting shape its edges; camera framing spends its area on space to inhabit.
- A distinctive branched canopy and constructed pavilion at the reachable
  overlook, with a framed wider city view. Local scale and distant scale differ.
- Graphic warm/cool lighting and materials with contact, coherent shadows and
  deliberate surface treatment. Finishing filters do not establish style.

These are construction directions, not mandatory exact geometry. The accepted
