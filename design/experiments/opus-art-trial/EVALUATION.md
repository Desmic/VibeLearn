# Observe the result, then compare intent

This is a bounded implementation-method trial. The old rejected Astra build is
a historical control, not an equivalent-context/time model benchmark. Do not
claim model superiority from unlike runs. A controlled head-to-head would require
both builders receiving the same packet, tools, limits and repair opportunities.
Do not spend on a second build merely to produce a leaderboard.

Preserve the initial build before repairs. Root can perform informed worker
inspection first. If the result merits integrated review, use one fresh-context
Astra reviewer under the existing critic workflow: play without the art brief or
creator explanation, record cold observations, then reveal intent/reference and
compare. All critic lanes remain independent judgments within that one session.
Use the existing schema-v2 review/presentation gates for new readiness records.

Record evidence against these questions, with pass/fail/unassessed and reasons:

1. Does the opening immediately invite exploration through a legible destination
   and depth? Is the path a useful inhabited place rather than a decorated floor?
2. Does the reference's graphic techno-fantasy identity survive camera changes?
   Inspect architecture/vegetation construction, silhouette, material response,
   light direction, contact shadow and the protagonist's fit with the world.
3. Does reaching the overlook deliver a new view and relational/world response?
   Can the observer describe inhabitants' purposes from what happened on screen?
4. Can the observer discover and perform Wake, travel, return, pause and restart?
   Probe early exploration, repeated activation and cancellation. Distinguish
   confusing presentation from a broken interaction. Assess portrait readability.
5. What did CPU/GPU frame times, frame pacing and rendering work actually show
   on the named device/settings? Check hidden/paused lifecycle separately.

Do not infer motion/audio quality, learning effectiveness, phone performance or
long-term engagement from screenshots or a completed route. This scene does not
teach/assess the LLM curriculum; those claims remain outside the art trial.

Compare the initial and final output, actual operator intervention, elapsed/model
usage and known limits. User art acceptance remains required. If the trial fails,
freeze it after the repair cap; do not quietly raise the scope/budget or call a
different style success. Extract reusable forms/materials/placement rules only
after a successful result. Preserve original source licenses and asset provenance.
